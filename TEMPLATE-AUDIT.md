# Premium fragrance template audit

Research date: 12 August 2026.

## Selected reference: Arome

ThemeForest's Arome Shopify 2.0 theme is the strongest functional reference from the current fragrance-specific set. It is recent, actively updated, rated 5.0 from four reviews at the time of review, and includes advanced media, quick view, wishlist, product comparison, lookbooks, shipping calculation and pre-order support.

What Aurum Privée carries forward as patterns:

- Campaign-scale fragrance photography
- A low-friction slide-out bag
- Product discovery before hard selling
- Wishlist-ready product cards
- Strong mobile product galleries
- Pickup and delivery clarity near checkout

What Aurum Privée does differently:

- A bespoke ivory, ink and antique-gold system built around the supplied Aurum Privée identity rather than a purchased theme
- Scent-family discovery written around mood and notes
- A custom Next.js integration layer designed around Loyverse
- Stripe, Resend and Supabase infrastructure that can be operated independently of a theme vendor

## Secondary references

- Perfomy: useful for merchandising density, product variation and collection navigation. It is a mature WooCommerce theme but is not the right technical base for a custom Loyverse integration.
- Cosmecos: useful for proven beauty-store category patterns and catalog breadth. Its visual language is more general cosmetics than fragrance.
- Keva: clean and fragrance-specific, but last updated in 2021 and less compelling as a 2026 foundation.

No ThemeForest source code, demo images or proprietary assets were copied. The implementation uses general interface patterns and an original design system.

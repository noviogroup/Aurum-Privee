# Aurum Privée content and copy pass

Prepared 23 September 2026 for the B2B quotation model. Implemented on the local `fix/b2b-rfq-launch-readiness` branch; not published.

## Positioning

Aurum Privée helps professional buyers source designer, niche and Arabian fragrance through a catalogue and direct quotation service. The website helps buyers compare products and prepare a request; the trade team confirms commercial terms privately.

Tone: clear, assured and helpful. Use British English: catalogue, programme, enquiry and fulfilment. Prefer concrete buyer actions over repeated references to a private commercial conversation.

## Key customer-facing copy

### Homepage

**Headline:** Wholesale fragrance for your business.

**Introduction:** Explore designer, niche and Arabian fragrance for retail, hospitality and corporate gifting. Build your selection and request pricing from our trade team.

**Actions:** Browse trade catalogue / How quoting works.

**Process introduction:** Choose the products your business needs. We’ll review your request and confirm pricing, availability and order requirements directly.

1. Explore the catalogue — Search by brand, fragrance or scent family. Compare sizes and concentrations, then add products to your quote list.
2. Set your quantities — Enter the number of units you need. Add notes about case packs, timing or special requirements.
3. Send your request — Share your business details and destination. We’ll review availability and prepare a quote, or contact you for more information.

### About

**Headline:** Fragrance for business.

**Introduction:** Aurum Privée helps retailers, hospitality teams and corporate buyers source fragrance through a trade catalogue and direct quotation service.

**Positioning line:** Build a fragrance range around the people you serve.

### Catalogue

**Headline:** Trade fragrance catalogue.

**Introduction:** Compare brands, sizes and concentrations. Add products to your quote list, then enter quantities and request wholesale pricing.

**Search label:** Search the catalogue.

### The Aurum Room

**Introduction:** Talk through your buying brief with the Aurum Privée trade team.

**Action:** Request a consultation.

The page describes a consultation service. It does not promise an appointment location, complimentary consultation, instant booking or a confirmed physical showroom.

### Quote list

**Introduction:** Review your products and quantities, then send your selection to the trade team for pricing and availability.

**Form introduction:** Tell us who you are buying for. We’ll review your selection and confirm pricing, minimums and availability directly.

**Confirmation:** Your quote request has been saved. Keep your reference above for follow-up. The trade team will review availability, quantities and commercial terms before responding.

**Boundary:** This request is not an order, reservation or binding quotation.

### Contact

**Introduction:** Ask about a quote, sourcing requirement or buyer consultation. Include your company name and any existing request reference so we can help.

**Action:** Send message.

**Confirmation heading:** Your message is with us.

### Footer and updates

Use Trade support instead of Client care; About Aurum Privée instead of Our story; Trade updates instead of Private list.

**Signup introduction:** Receive catalogue news and product updates by email. Confirm your subscription in your inbox.

## Other improvements

- Product-page instructions explain that quantity entry follows selection; pricing, minimums and availability require a quote.
- Product references say “Fragrance details from” instead of implying the source has verified this website.
- Search and empty-state text explain what happened and what the buyer can do next.
- Technical configuration failures return understandable customer messages.
- Contact, newsletter and RFQ email templates use the same trade language. No live emails were sent.
- The RFQ acknowledgement tells buyers to keep their reference; the merchant template explains the manual follow-up action.
- Privacy, trade terms and fulfilment copy describe the actual service and chosen operating defaults. Policy pages retain existing noindex settings while merchant identity, retention and transaction-specific details are completed.
- Authenticity copy invites sourcing documentation requests without inventing authorised-distributor status or verified supply-chain relationships.

## Content boundaries

The copy pass covers shared customer-facing pages, navigation, forms, response messages, product-page framing and trade email templates. It does not independently verify or rewrite the full product catalogue. Official product descriptions, sizes, concentrations, scent notes and image rights still require source-by-source catalogue QA. The lower-resolution product assets identified in the visual review have not been replaced.

Do not add claims of exclusive distribution, official brand partnerships, guaranteed stock, worldwide delivery, guaranteed response times or company history without merchant evidence.

## Operating defaults

See [B2B-OPERATING-DEFAULTS.md](./B2B-OPERATING-DEFAULTS.md). The seven-day quote validity is a chosen default; actual minimums, case packs, shipping and final transaction terms belong in each written quote.
